Creature Spawner 1.0.0 - como compilar (feito com o csc.dll do SDK do dotnet, Windows)

Referencias (NAO incluidas aqui, sao do jogo): copiar da pasta do Hollow Knight, hollow_knight_Data\Managed\:
  Assembly-CSharp.dll (a versao do Modding API), PlayMaker.dll, mscorlib.dll, netstandard.dll, System.dll, System.Core.dll,
  UnityEngine.dll, UnityEngine.CoreModule.dll, UnityEngine.AssetBundleModule.dll, UnityEngine.IMGUIModule.dll,
  UnityEngine.InputLegacyModule.dll, UnityEngine.Physics2DModule.dll
  -> colocar numa pasta "refs".

Comando (Git Bash), dentro desta pasta:
  dotnet "<SDK>\Roslyn\bincore\csc.dll" -nologo -target:library -out:CreatureSpawner.dll -nostdlib -langversion:latest \
      -r:refs\Assembly-CSharp.dll ... (todas as DLLs acima) src\*.cs

Instalar: copiar CreatureSpawner.dll (+ ReadMe.md) para
  Hollow Knight\hollow_knight_Data\Managed\Mods\CreatureSpawner\   (so com o jogo fechado)

Chave util: src\BrokenVesselFix.cs -> KeepFlyingScuttlers (false = original; true = bichinhos arremessados).
